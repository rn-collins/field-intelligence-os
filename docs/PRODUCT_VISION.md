# Product Vision

## One-sentence vision

Field Intelligence OS gives investigators one place to plan, capture, verify, connect, retrieve and transform multimodal field reality into trustworthy outputs.

## The user experience

A user opens one command center rather than searching across chats, spreadsheets, folders, notes and editing applications. From a deployment, the user can see the people, institutions, interviews, claims, evidence, laws, systems, assets, permissions, gaps, commitments and outputs connected to that work.

## What makes the product distinct

- It treats media as evidence-linked field intelligence rather than an undifferentiated media library.
- It records investigator cognition and hypothesis change.
- It reconstructs systems through actors, rules, handoffs, exceptions and consequences.
- It supports role separation and provenance from capture through publication.
- It combines field journalism, legal research, science communication, documentary practice, design research and knowledge graphs.

## First release success

The first usable release succeeds when RN can plan and execute Manhattan and Reykjavík inside the system, capture minimum packages on mobile, preserve consent, connect claims to evidence, see missing coverage, and assemble source-backed outputs without duplicating data across six trackers.
