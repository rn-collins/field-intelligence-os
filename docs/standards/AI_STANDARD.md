# AI Standard

- AI assistance is advisory unless a feature explicitly requires human confirmation.
- Generated statements must identify supporting internal records or say evidence is unavailable.
- Never invent quotations, consent, legal status, scientific certainty or source identity.
- Preserve the source transcript and media master behind every extracted claim.
- Track model/provider, prompt version, time and reviewer for consequential AI operations.
- Sensitive records must not be sent to external models without configured authorization and disclosure.
- Provide an undo or rejection path for AI-proposed metadata and links.
