# Security and Privacy Standard

Treat unpublished reporting, source identity, health information, consent, off-record material, legal matters and precise sensitive locations as protected data.

- Least privilege by default.
- RLS and server-side authorization are mandatory.
- Secrets live only in approved local and deployment secret stores.
- Encrypt data in transit and rely on provider encryption at rest; evaluate field-level encryption for high-risk records.
- Audit access and material permission changes.
- Support redaction, restricted records, retention rules and incident response.
- Security findings are reported privately under `SECURITY.md`, never in public issues.
